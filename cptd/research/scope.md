## Exam facts (handbook)

- Weights are given only per domain: 20 / 45 / 35. No per-capability weights or item counts.
- The outline counts 150 "measurement opportunities" for the whole exam (90 multiple-choice questions plus 10 case scenarios of 3–4 steps). At 45%, Domain 2 is worth about 40 multiple-choice questions and a matching share of case steps; Domain 3 about 32.
- Statement counts: Domain 1 has 11 (1.1.A … 1.6.A), **Domain 2 has 18**, **Domain 3 has 32**.

## Wording note

The fetch tool would only quote statements under about 125 characters word for word. Longer ones are given as the quoted core plus **every** named example in printed order, which is what matters for content (Domain 1's lesson: don't drop a named element). Paraphrased tails are marked *(tail paraphrased)*. Recheck a long statement's tail against the PDF before using it as a label.
